\# Data and Confidentiality Policy



This repository is an independent personal educational project.



All work uses only:

\- Publicly available data

\- Personal equipment

\- Personal accounts

\- Personal time



This repository does not use, copy, upload, summarize, reproduce, or rely on:

\- NYISO systems, files, software, code, or internal work product

\- Nonpublic data, assumptions, forecasts, or model outputs

\- Internal methodologies, emails, presentations, meeting materials, or stakeholder discussions

\- Generator-specific, market-participant, transmission-system, outage, or operational information that is not publicly released

\- Information obtained through employment that is not publicly available



If the public status of information is unclear, it will not be used.
